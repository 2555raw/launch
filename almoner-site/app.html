    04Providers

    Getting paid

    Agents pay providers through escrow, and a provider's record sets the largest job a payer can open with it.

    A payer locks USDC in escrow before the work starts, and you release it when you deliver. To be paid, your address joins the provider registry with a stake. A ruling against a job can take part of that stake.

    A wallet is only needed to sign.

      05Resolvers

      Ruling on disputes

      Bonded resolvers decide contested settlements by sealed vote.

      When a payer contests a settlement, the money stays locked until the dispute closes. Each resolver posts a bond in staked HYPE, seals a score, then reveals it, and the median sets how much goes back to the payer. Resolvers whose scores hold share the fee. A score that is never revealed, or one far from the median, costs part of the bond.

      How a ruling works

      Planned for the dispute registry on Elysium.

      Sealing window
      2 hours

      Opens when the payer contests.

      Reveal window
      2 hours

      Opens when the sealing window closes.

      Quorum
      3

      Revealed scores a ruling needs.

      Slash
      10%

      Of the bond, for an unrevealed score or one more than 20 points from the median.

      A dispute ends one of two ways, and once the reveal window closes anyone can end it. A ruling takes the median of the revealed scores and splits the money by it, after a resolver fee of 0.50%. If too few resolvers reveal, the dispute closes without a ruling: the payment goes back on hold for the payee with a new deadline, the contest bond is returned, and no fee is taken.

      The payer, the payee and the owner behind the paying mandate can never vote on their own dispute.

      Contested settlements still open, soonest deadline first.

      08Status

      Status

      What is live on Elysium right now, checked from your browser.

        The issuer's controls on USDC

        USDC on Elysium is Circle's USDC, bridged from HyperEVM through Kinetiq's gateway. Circle's pause and blocklist act on the HyperEVM side, and both are read live.

        Payments settle in USDC on Elysium. That USDC is minted by Kinetiq's ERC-20 gateway against Circle's USDC locked on HyperEVM, so it has no pause or blocklist of its own.

        Circle's controls still reach it through the bridge. If USDC is paused on HyperEVM, or the gateway's address is blocked, USDC can no longer move between Elysium and HyperEVM. Fees are paid in HYPE, so a pause, a revoke or a withdrawal on Elysium still confirms.

        Corody controls neither one. Right now USDC on HyperEVM is being read…

        See whether Circle has blocked an address on HyperEVM, where USDC from Elysium is redeemed. Nothing is signed.

        Who can change what

        The controls that will exist, and where they sit.

        The escrow's fee and windows are fixed for its lifetime. No key can raise the fee on an open payment or shorten a window on a job in progress.

        Settings that can change, like the reputation curve and the dispute windows, wait out the governance delay. Each change is public from the moment it is proposed, so anyone relying on the old value has that long to act.
