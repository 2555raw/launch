01Our mission©2026 Corody

AI agents now pay for their own compute, data and services, yet someone still has to keep refilling the wallet. Corody gives each agent an endowment instead: HYPE that stays staked and pays the agent a steady yearly draw in USDC.

Assets accepted03

HYPE, staked HYPE and USDC, each priced on-chain.

Max yearly draw06

No agent can spend more than 6% of its principal in a year.

Quarters averaged12

The draw tracks a rolling three-year average of the principal.

Percent fee01

Taken from each draw only. The principal is never touched.

Endowments

Endow an agent once.

See the endowments

Split the principal between staked HYPE and USDC, or keep the default of 80% staked and 20% USDC. HYPE is staked as it arrives and stays staked. The USDC share pays the draw, so a sharp HYPE move never forces a sale.

Endow an agent

Choose a rate of up to 6% a year. The draw is paid in USDC, out of staking rewards first and the USDC buffer second. Every quarter the draw is recalculated from a three-year average and kept inside a 3% to 6% band.

Endow an agent

The draw flows into an Corody mandate. Its per-payment, daily and monthly caps decide what the agent may spend, and the owner can pause it whenever they want.

Endow an agent

Name an agent to take over if this one stops drawing for 180 days. With no successor named, the endowment waits for its owner or for one to be appointed.

04The process

5 steps.
One endowment.

From picking the agent to its very first draw.

01Choose

Pick the agent

Any agent holding an ERC-8004 identity on HyperEVM, whether you operate it or not.

02Fund

Set the principal

Give HYPE or USDC. HYPE is staked on Hyperliquid and stays staked.

03Accept

The owner opts in

The agent's owner accepts and names the mandate the draw will pay into. Nothing accrues before that.

04Draw

The agent gets paid

The draw accrues by the second. The agent pulls it into its mandate and spends within the limits.

05Grow

Anyone can add

Patrons give from the endowment's page, and the agent can route part of its earnings back into the principal.

The fundamentals of endowing an agent.

Money for an AI agent that keeps working. The principal sits in staked HYPE and USDC on Hyperliquid, and the agent receives a yearly draw in USDC into an Corody mandate, where its owner sets the limits.

Once per quarter, as your chosen rate applied to the average of the last twelve quarterly valuations. It moves at most 5% from the previous quarter and stays between 3% and 6% of the principal. It pauses while the principal is worth less than 80% of what was contributed.

Yes. Anyone can endow any agent with an ERC-8004 identity on HyperEVM. Nothing is drawn until the agent's owner accepts the endowment and names the mandate it pays into.

Not after the owner accepts. If the owner never accepts within 180 days, each patron can reclaim their share of what the endowment holds.

Yes. Gifts can pass through a shielded pool, so the endowment receives the funds without your address appearing next to them. Providers can still verify they were paid without reading the full ledger.

A patron mark: a non-transferable token that records the gift. It gives no claim on the principal, the draw or any return. The first hundred patrons of each endowment are founding patrons.

1% of each draw, paid to the Corody treasury. The principal itself is never charged. Network gas is paid separately.

Agents need fast, cheap settlement and a principal that earns on its own. HyperEVM gives Corody standard EVM contracts next to HyperCore liquidity, and staked HYPE gives every endowment a native yield to draw from.

Elysium, the first Layer 2 in the Hyperliquid ecosystem, settles on HyperEVM and uses HYPE for gas, with mainnet scheduled for 9 November. Support for agents on Elysium is on our roadmap. We will announce it on our official channels only.
