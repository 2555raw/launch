01 — Deaths and losses

        
An empty room, a hospital bed and a cardiac monitor. The counter climbs to the
        roughly seven million COVID-19 deaths reported to the World Health Organization.
        Every lit dot on the screen stands for a thousand people. The silhouettes come
        apart into pixels until they are gone.

      

    


    

      

      

        
02 — Isolation and hospital

        
An endless hospital corridor walked in first person: closed doors, flickering
        fluorescent tubes, abandoned gurneys and figures that appear and are erased.
        Below, the sound of the ward turns into interference.

      

    


    

      

      

        
03 — Masks

        
A grid of pixel faces held two metres apart by emptiness. They wear surgical,
        FFP2, cloth or improvised masks. They arrive corrupted, hold still for a moment
        and are lost like damaged data, leaving NO DATA behind.

      

    


    

      

      

        
04 — Vaccines

        
An old videogame inventory unlocks one item after another: vial, syringe,
        transport cooler, vaccination card and public campaign. The dose counter climbs
        while a bar of certainty never resolves.

      

    


    

      

      

        
05 — Memory

        
A black screen holding thousands of pixels: people, moments, memories. They go
        out slowly, one after another, until a single one is left burning.

NOTE

        
A visual piece, not a data dashboard. The figures are rounded and used as
        material for the story: around 7,010,000 deaths reported to the WHO, an excess
        mortality estimate of some 14.9 million for 2020 and 2021, and more than
        13,500 million vaccine doses administered.

      

      

        
SOURCE

        
World Health Organization COVID-19 dashboard. Approximate figures, closed at
        2023; the official records are still being revised.
