Our kitchen

The name comes from the bowl where it all starts.

An asanka is the ridged clay bowl Ghanaian kitchens use to grind peppers, tomato and onion. It is the base of every soup and every jollof we serve.

We pound the fufu every morning from cassava and plantain, never instant flour. The soups cook for hours, and the jollof finishes in a heavy pot until the rice toasts on the bottom: that smoky party flavour everyone is after.

  • Fresh produceRoots, peppers and meat bought every day.
  • Slow cookingFour-hour soups, no shortcuts, no stock cubes.
  • Family recipesLearned in Kumasi and Lagos, cooked here.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What is fufu and how do you eat it?

It is a soft, stretchy dough of pounded cassava and plantain. Pinch off a piece with your right hand, dip it in the soup and swallow it without much chewing. We add cutlery if you prefer.

How far do you deliver?

We deliver within 8 km of the kitchen. When you order, we confirm on WhatsApp that your address is in range and when it will arrive.

How spicy is the jollof?

Our base jollof is mild and fragrant. Choose mild, medium or properly hot, and add shito, our pepper and dried shrimp sauce, on the side.

Do you have vegan or gluten-free options?

Fufu and jollof are naturally gluten-free, and so is the whole menu. The vegan section lists the dishes with no animal products. Tell us about any allergy in the order notes.

How do I pay?

By cash, card or bank transfer when your order arrives. For catering we ask for a 30% deposit to confirm the date.
