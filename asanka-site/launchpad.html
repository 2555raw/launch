    How it works

    From the pot to graduation

    1. 1

      Pair it with a dish

      Choose one of sixteen dishes, give the coin a name and a ticker, and say why it matters. Launching costs 0.02 SOL. You can make the first buy yourself.

    2. 2

      Trade on the curve

      There is no presale and no team allocation. All billion tokens sit in a bonding curve: each buy raises the price, each sell lowers it, with a 1% fee.

    3. 3

      Graduate

      When the curve has raised its SOL, the coin reaches a market cap and graduates. On a live launchpad the liquidity would then move to a DEX.

    This page is a demo of how such a launchpad works. It does not connect to Solana or any other chain, it does not hold funds, and the coins do not exist outside your browser. A real launch needs audited contracts and a legal review in every country where it is offered.
