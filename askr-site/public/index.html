FAQ

Everything you need to know: models, funding, privacy and what it costs.

What is Askr?

Askr is a single platform where you can access every major AI model for text, image, video and audio, paying only for what you use.

Do I need a subscription?

No. There is nothing recurring. You fund one balance and it goes down as you use models. When it runs out you top up, and only then.

How do I pay?

With crypto from the wallet you already use: USDT, BTC, ETH or SOL, at the live rate when you fund. Or by card through Stripe. Your deposit is converted to credits at $1 = 1,000 credits with no askr fee on the deposit.

What models are available?

Chat and code with Claude, GPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, Grok and open models like Llama, Qwen and Mistral. Images with FLUX and Nano Banana. Video with Kling, Seedance, Veo and Runway. Voice with GPT-4o TTS and ElevenLabs. New models are added as they ship, and $ASKR holders get them 14 days early.

How much does it cost?

Each model has a transparent per-request price on the pricing page, metered against your balance as you go: per token for text, per image, per second of video. The calculator turns your workload into credits. $ASKR holders pay 5% of the list price within a daily allowance.

Is my data private?

There is no email and no sign-up form: an account is an access key or a wallet signature. Prompts go to the model provider you chose and nowhere else. Your chats and library stay on your account until you delete them.

Do my credits expire?

No. Credits stay on your balance until you spend them.
