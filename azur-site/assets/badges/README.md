Official store badges go here, and the download buttons on the home page switch to them
automatically:

- `app-store.svg`: Apple's "Download on the App Store" badge, from Apple's App Store
  marketing guidelines page (developer.apple.com/app-store/marketing/guidelines/).
- `google-play.svg`: Google's "Get it on Google Play" badge, from Google Play's badge
  page (play.google.com/intl/en_us/badges/).

Both companies only allow the badges once the app is live in their store, and each
badge should link to the app's store listing. Until then, the buttons show the
placeholder phone and play icons.
