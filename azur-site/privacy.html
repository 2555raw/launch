Privacy Policy

Draft for review. Have a lawyer check this before launch.

What we collect

  • Account details: email, name and device identifiers.
  • Connected accounts: balances, positions and transaction history you authorize us to read.
  • Conversations: the messages you send the agent and the files in your workspace.
  • Usage data: crash reports and basic analytics.

How we use it

To run the app, answer your questions, send the alerts you ask for, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations. We do not sell your personal data.

Who we share it with

Service providers that host and process data for us, the brokerages and networks you connect, and authorities when the law requires it. Onchain transactions are public by nature.

Retention

We keep your data while your account is open. Delete your account from Settings and we remove your conversations and workspace within 30 days, except records we must keep by law.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data. Email hello@example.com and we will reply within 30 days.
