The mechanism

Every trade fills the basket

A Ballast coin is a normal coin with one extra part: a vault that turns trading fees into stocks and pays them out. When volume stops, the basket stops filling. We say that up front.

  1. TradeAnyone buys or sells the coin
  2. 1% fee
  3. VaultSplits the fee on-chain, same rule for every coin
  4. 60%
  5. BasketBuys the coin's 3–5 tokenized stocks at their weights
  6. every 15 min
  7. HoldersStocks land in wallets pro rata. No claim, no staking

Step 1

Pick the basket

Choose 3 to 5 tokenized stocks and their weights. The basket is written into the coin at launch and can't be edited later.

Step 2

Launch in one transaction

1B supply goes straight to a pool with locked liquidity. No presale, no team allocation, no snipers: the buy fee starts high and decays to 1% in the first blocks.

Step 3

Trades fill it

Every trade pays 1%. The vault buys the basket and pays holders every 15 minutes, with a link to each payout on the explorer.

Agents

Launch from your agent, inside limits it can't break

Ballast speaks the formats agents already use: an MCP server, a skill file and a one-line command. You set the spend limits once; a contract enforces them and anything outside reverts.

{
  "tool": "ballast.launch_coin",
  "input": {
    "name": "Orbit",
    "ticker": "ORBIT",
    "chain": "robinhood",
    "basket": { "NVDA": 40, "TSLA": 30, "SPY": 30 },
    "max_spend_eth": 0.05
  }
}
!ballast launch ORBIT basket=NVDA:40,TSLA:30,SPY:30 chain=robinhood

→ validated in <1 min
→ pool live from block 1, liquidity locked
→ basket vault armed, first payout in 15 min
# Ballast skill

Rules

What can't change after launch

  • 1% fee, forever.There is no owner function to raise it. No taxes that can be edited into a honeypot.
  • No team allocation.All 1B tokens go to the pool. The creator earns only from trading fees.
  • Liquidity locked.Locked at launch. Nobody can pull it.
  • Basket fixed.Stocks and weights are written into the coin and shown on its page.
  • Every payout on the explorer.Each 15-minute payout links to its transaction.
  • No promise of returns.Payouts depend on volume. Stocks can fall. No volume, no basket.

Official BLST address

Ballast has not launched a token. Any $BLST you see today is not ours. The address will be published here, and only here, at launch.

FAQ

Straight answers

Is this yield?

No. A Ballast coin is a coin with a mechanic: trading fees buy stocks for holders. There is no promised return. If nobody trades, nothing is bought, and the stocks in the basket can lose value.

Can the fee or the basket change later?

No. Both are set at launch and there is no function to edit them.

Do I need to claim or stake?

No. Payouts go straight to holders' wallets every 15 minutes, pro rata to their balance at payout time.

How do you stop snipers?

The buy fee starts high in the first blocks and decays to 1% on a public schedule. Every coin uses the same schedule.

What does BLST do?

0.10% of every trade on every coin buys BLST and burns it. It gets no other promises.

Can my agent launch without me?

Only inside the limits you set. Actions outside them revert, and "Revoke all" turns every delegation off in one call.

Where is it available?

Tokenized stocks aren't available everywhere. The app checks your region before you can buy a coin.
