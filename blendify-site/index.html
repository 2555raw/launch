HOW IT WORKS

From a blank basket to a live market.

FAQ

The questions traders ask before their first index.

Short answers, straight from how the protocol works.

How many assets can one index hold?

Three to five. The weights have to add up to 100%, and they are stored on-chain, so the composition does not drift between trades.

Do I keep custody of my funds?

Yes. Deposits, spot positions and perp settlement all run on-chain from your own wallet, and you withdraw straight back to it.

Which chains and asset types are supported?

Robinhood Chain, Ethereum, Base, BNB Chain and Solana. Tokens, tokenized stocks such as tTSLA and tNVDA, and real-world assets such as tGOLD can sit in the same index.

How often does the blended price update?

Every ten seconds, weighted across every leg, from DexScreener and equity feeds.

How much leverage can I take?

Up to 5x through perps, long or short on the whole index, with the liquidation level shown before you open the position.

What does it cost to launch an index?
