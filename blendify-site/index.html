How it works

Four steps, no listing queue

No approval committee and no listing fee. A bag goes live the moment you save it, and the weights you set are the weights it trades at.

01

Pick the assets

Three to five of them: BTC, ETH, NVDA, TSLA, gold or oil, from any supported chain.

02

Set the weights

Tune each weight until they add to 100%. Stored onchain, so the bag cannot drift.

03

Buy it in one trade

A single order fills every leg at a blended price, refreshed every ten seconds.

04

Or trade it with leverage

Long or short the whole basket through perps, with the liquidation level shown up front.

FAQ

The short answers

How many assets can one bag hold?

Three to five. The weights have to add up to 100%, and they are stored onchain, so the bag does not drift between trades.

Do I keep custody of my funds?

Yes. Deposits, spot positions and perp settlement all run onchain from your own wallet, and you withdraw straight back to it.

Which chains and asset types are supported?

Robinhood Chain, Ethereum, Base, BNB Chain and Solana. Tokens, tokenized stocks such as tTSLA and tNVDA, and real world assets such as tGOLD can sit in the same index.

How often does the blended price update?

Every ten seconds, weighted across every leg, from DexScreener and equity feeds.

How much leverage can I take?

Up to 5x through perps, long or short on the whole bag, with the liquidation level shown before you open the position.

What does it cost to launch a bag?

There is no listing fee and no approval queue: it goes live the moment you save it. Trading fees are [your fee schedule].
