What Clematis is

Clematis is a website that helps you build swaps on Robinhood Chain. It reads prices from public Uniswap v3 and v4 pools and prepares a transaction that you review and sign in your own wallet. The swap itself runs through Uniswap's Universal Router.

Clematis never holds your funds or keys, can't move tokens on your behalf, and can't cancel, reverse or refund a transaction once it is on-chain. It doesn't issue, own or control any token, pool or smart contract shown on the site.

What a tokenized stock is

The stock tokens on Robinhood Chain are issued by a third party, not by Clematis and not by the companies they track. They give economic exposure to a share's price. Holding one is not holding the share: you get no voting rights and no legal claim on the company. Check the issuer's own documents for the exact terms of each token.

Risks

  • Price. Token prices move with the market and with on-chain trading. You can lose some or all of what you put in.
  • Liquidity. Some pools are small. A large swap can move the price a lot (price impact), and a quote can change before your swap confirms. Your slippage setting caps how much less you'll accept; if the price moves past it, the swap fails instead.
  • Tracking. A token's on-chain price can differ from the share price on a stock exchange, especially when that exchange is closed.
  • Smart contracts and networks. Tokens, pools, routers, bridges and Robinhood Chain itself are software and can have bugs, be paused, or fail.
  • Your wallet. Anyone with your keys or seed phrase controls your funds. Transactions sent to the wrong address can't be recovered.
  • Bridges. Bridges listed on Clematis are run by third parties. Use them at your own risk.

Fees

Clematis charges no platform fee. Each swap pays the pool's liquidity fee, shown before you confirm, and the Robinhood Chain network fee.
