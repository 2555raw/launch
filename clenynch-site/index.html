The Bench Code

Six rules, written in crumbs, agreed to by nobody but Clenynch.

TheBenchCode.rtf — TextEdit
1

Never open your eyes while eating.

What you can't see can't ask for a bite.

2

Every bench is your bench.

If you're sitting on it, it's occupied. Permanently.

3

Sharing is a suggestion.

A polite one. Very easy to ignore.

4

Crumbs are not currency.

They're a rounding error. Nobody's getting change.

5

Silence is a negotiating tactic.

If nobody says anything, nothing has to be decided.

6

The last bite decides everything.

How the story ends depends entirely on what Clenynch does with it.
