Questions

Ask the chef

Is this a game or a token?

Both, and that's the point. Order Rush is a real cooking game you can play in the browser or the app. $SIZZLE is what the kitchen pays you in. You can play without ever touching a wallet; the coins only leave the game when you decide to connect one.

Where do the payouts come from?

From the community slice (25% of supply) and from the 1% of every swap that goes into the tip jar. When the community slice runs out, payouts come from tips only, so they scale with how much the kitchen is used.

Do I need a chef NFT to play?

No. Everyone starts with the default chef. NFT chefs earn a tip multiplier (1.1× to 2×) and unlock cosmetic kitchens, but they never change how the game plays.

Is the liquidity locked?

Yes, for 12 months from launch, and the team allocation vests linearly over 24 months. Both are verifiable on-chain; the links are on the wallet page.

Can I lose money?

Yes. $SIZZLE is a volatile asset and can go to zero. Play the game for fun, buy the token only with what you are comfortable burning.
