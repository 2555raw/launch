01 — Mechanics

What the vault
actually does

Four steps, and only the first one is yours.

  1. 1

    You deposit USDG

    The router swaps half of it into the Stock Token and joins the pool in a single transaction. Whatever is left over goes straight back to your wallet.

  2. 2

    You get vault shares

    A plain ERC-4626 share. It is your claim on the position, it moves with the Stock Token price, and it is the thing you redeem later.

  3. 3

    The position stays centred

    Liquidity sits in a tight band around the oracle price. Once the market walks out of that band, a rebalance is queued and checked against fresh feeds before it runs.

  4. 4

    Fees compound in place

    Trading fees are collected and folded back into the position, so the share price rises rather than paying anything out. Redeem whenever you want.

02 — Fees

Where the
fees land

Every fee the position earns is split the same way, on every collection.

  • 70% compounds back into the position — this is the part that lifts the share price.
  • 20% buys back CUSP on the open market and burns it.
  • 10% goes to the protocol treasury: audits, oracles, keepers.

There is no deposit fee, no management fee and no lock-up. The only cost of leaving is the gas and the pool's own swap fee on the way out.

03 — Safeguards

What stops
a bad day

Each vault is capped, pausable and auditable on its own.

Stale feeds wait

Chainlink price and sequencer feeds must be fresh before any rebalance or borrow. When they are not, deposits and rebalances wait instead of guessing. Redemptions stay open the whole time.

One vault, one blast radius

Vaults, markets and strategies are separate contracts with separate caps. A vault that has to be paused is paused alone; nothing else in the protocol stops.

Caps, not hope

Every vault carries a hard deposit cap. When it fills, deposits close until the cap is raised in the open — the capacity bar on each row is that cap.

Verified, byte for byte

Every contract is verified on Blockscout against the reviewed deployment, so the code you read is the code holding the position.

04 — Questions

Before you
deposit

The five that matter most.

Is my principal protected?

No. A vault share is a claim on a liquidity position, so its value moves with the Stock Token price and with the fees the position earns. If the stock falls, the share falls with it — the fees soften that, they do not cancel it.

What does “in range” mean on a row?

That the oracle price is inside the band where the vault's liquidity sits, which is the only state in which the position earns fees. “Rebalancing” means the price has walked out and a re-centre is queued behind a fresh-feed check.

When can I withdraw?

Any block. Redeeming burns your shares, pulls your slice of the position and returns USDG. Redemptions are deliberately the last thing a pause or a stale feed touches.

Why does the APR keep moving?

Because it is a 24-hour observation, annualised. It follows how much was actually traded in that pool yesterday, and a quiet day drops it as fast as a busy one lifts it.

What am I depositing, exactly?

USDG, and only USDG. The router handles the swap into the Stock Token and the join in one transaction, and returns the dust.
