Placeholder copy for a design mock. Cusp is not a real protocol, none of this has been near a lawyer, and none of it is legal advice. It is here to show what belongs on the page — anything shipping for real needs its own drafting and its own review.

There is no account

No sign-up, no email, no password, no profile. Nothing on this site asks who you are, and there is no server here that could remember you if it did.

What this page stores on your device

Two things, both in your browser’s local storage, both readable only by this page, neither sent anywhere:

  • cusp-theme — whether you picked the light or the dark theme.
  • cusp-demo — the demo wallet: a random address, a play-money balance and the positions you opened with it.

Clear your site data and both are gone. Block storage entirely and the page still works; it simply forgets.

The demo wallet is not a wallet

Its address is random, its balance is play money, and it never leaves your browser. Nobody — here or anywhere else — has a copy, and no chain has ever heard of it.

The one third-party request

The page loads Inter and JetBrains Mono from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com, and those servers see your IP address and user agent — as they do on any site that uses them.

That is the only external request the page makes. Self-hosting the two fonts removes it, and is the right move before any real launch.

No analytics, no cookies, no trackers

This build sets no cookies and loads no analytics, no tag manager, no pixel and no advertising script. If that ever changes, this page changes with it — and the change belongs in the banner, not in a footnote.

The chain is public, permanently

Anything signed on-chain is public and permanent by design: your address, every deposit, every redemption, every balance, readable by anyone forever and linkable to whatever else that address has ever done.

That is a property of the chain, not of this site, and no privacy policy can undo it.

Wallets and RPC providers

A real deployment talks to your wallet extension and to an RPC provider, and both see things this page does not: your IP address and the requests your wallet makes. Their policies are theirs, and worth reading.
