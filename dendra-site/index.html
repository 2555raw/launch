Features

Built for the Bittensor economy, not bolted on

Launchpads elsewhere pair with whatever gas token is lying around. Dendra is designed around TAO and subnet alpha from the start.

Native TAO & alpha pairs

Every buy routes value into TAO or the subnet's alpha, so your launch strengthens the network it lives on.

Curve to pool graduation

Once the curve fills, liquidity migrates to a Uniswap-style pool automatically and the LP is locked.

Fund from anywhere

Holding USDC on Base? Bridge and swap into TAO inside the same flow; no second app, no copy-pasted addresses.

Creator fee share

A slice of every trade goes to the launcher's wallet, forever, so good theses pay the people who write them.

Fair by default

No presale, no hidden allocation, no admin mint. Everyone buys on the same curve, including the creator.

Live chain data

Prices, holders and curve progress are read straight from the chain, not from a cache that lags behind.

FAQ

Before you sign

Still unsure? Read the docs or ask in the community channel.

What can I launch?

Anything you can describe in a sentence: a subnet, a person, a prediction, an in-joke. Tokens impersonating real projects or people without permission are delisted from the front end.

How does the bonding curve work?

Price rises as supply is bought and falls as it's sold, according to a fixed formula. There's always a quote, so there's always an exit, until the curve fills and graduates to a pool.

What happens at graduation?

When the curve reaches its target, the collected TAO or alpha and the remaining supply are deposited into a pool and the LP tokens are locked. Trading continues there.

How much does it cost?

Deploying costs network gas plus a small flat fee. Each trade carries a 1% fee, part of which goes to the creator.

Are the contracts audited?

An audit is in progress and the report will be published here when it's done. Until then, treat Dendra as beta software and only use what you can afford to lose.

Which wallet do I need?

Any EVM wallet that can add a custom network: MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet and OKX Wallet are tested. The app adds Bittensor EVM for you.
