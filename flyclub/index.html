How the shift works

Customers come to the bar and ask for a beer, a cocktail, a martini or a shot. Pour the right one before their patience runs out. Correct pours build a combo and the crowd's hype; a wrong pour spills the glass and resets it.

Hype & dopamine

Fill the hype bar and the club calls a SHOT ROUND: everyone wants shots, tips double and the lasers lose their mind. The dopamine bar fills as you serve, and at 100% the fly goes into NEURAL OVERDRIVE.
