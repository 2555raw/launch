## 1 — the launch, with the reel attached

An AI you can say anything to, that keeps none of it. No thread, no history,

hanamy.xyz

## 2 — the proof, with a screenshot of the receipt
Every chat says "we don't store your messages".

Hanamy prints the request instead. The JSON under the panel is the same object
that was posted, and the messages array in it has one entry. Not a description
of what gets sent. The thing that was sent.

## 3 — the audit, with a screenshot of the two rows
"What do you keep about me" should have a boring answer.

Hanamy's: two things, both on your device. Which colour mode you picked, and
which endpoint you chose. The page reads your browser and lists them. One
button clears both.

A conversation is never on that list.

## 5 — the limit, said out loud
The part most privacy pages leave out:

Hanamy keeps nothing. What the provider does with a request after it arrives is
the provider's business, and no web page can promise otherwise.

The guarantee stops at the browser. Anyone claiming further is selling
something.

## 6 — free

Google AI Studio, OpenRouter's free tier, all without a card. Or the site
carries one and asks you for nothing.
