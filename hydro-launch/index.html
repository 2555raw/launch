01

Pick the source

32 contracts across reservoirs, aquifers, glaciers and desalination. Each one carries its venue, its quality assay and its unit of measure.

02

The token inherits it

The opening price comes from the source's spot and its fill. The less water there is to go round, the dearer the token opens.

03

Curve to graduation

Trading runs on a bonding curve up to 4.2 ETH. Once it fills, the pool opens and the token is released.

04

The vault takes its cut

3% of every trade goes into the token's own vault. Nothing is distributed: it accumulates for as long as the pairing lives.

Hydro — a demo. No chain, no wallet, no value: everything is simulated in your browser.
