What a pairing is, and what it is not

Pairing a coin to a reserve writes that reserve's ticker into the ERC-20, where anyone can read it back on chain. That is the whole of it. It conveys no ownership of the water, no legal claim on it, no abstraction right and no physical backing, and it is not an agreement with the operator named on the entry. Nothing here becomes a claim on real water unless a separate, verified legal structure exists, and none does.

The figures in the register are published reference data for the named body of water. They label the pairing. They are not a price feed and the curve does not trade on them: a coin's price comes from the bonding curve in the contract and from nothing else.
