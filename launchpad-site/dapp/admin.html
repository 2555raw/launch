The launchpad reads the KuaiFactory contract (contracts/KuaiLaunch.sol) on the chain the page uses. Deploy it from here with the server's DEPLOYER_KEY (the wallet needs a little BNB for gas: about 0.05 tBNB on testnet) or deploy it yourself with npm run deploy -- --chain 97 and paste the address. Addresses can also come from FACTORY_56 / FACTORY_97 on the server; a value saved here wins. The merchant wallet for gift cards is MERCHANT_ADDRESS.

Staking ($KUAI)

KuaiStaking receives the 1% graduation fee: 70% is paid in BNB to $KUAI stakers, 30% buys back $KUAI and burns it. Deploying it also points the factory's feeTo at it when the deployer owns the factory (only coins created afterwards pay into it). Set the house coin once it exists (launch $KUAI on kuai itself, or paste any BEP-20). Harvest converts stock-token fees (MCDx…) to BNB; buyback spends the pool. Both swap on PancakeSwap with a 3–5% minimum, signed by DEPLOYER_KEY.
