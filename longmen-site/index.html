Before you sign

Is the token I launch subnet alpha?

No. You're launching your own ERC-20 that trades against TAO or a subnet's alpha. It doesn't register a subnet or receive emissions.

What actually happens when I launch?

One transaction creates the token, writes its metadata and opens its market: a bonding curve on Robinhood Chain, or a locked pool seeded by you on Bittensor EVM.

How much does it cost?

Network gas, plus the initial reserve you choose on Bittensor EVM. Trades pay a small fee, part of which goes to the creator.

Can I take the starting liquidity back out?

No. Pool liquidity is sent to an address nobody controls, which is what lets traders trust the market won't be pulled.

Do I need to sign up?

No account and no email. Your wallet is your login.

Has the code been audited?

Not yet by an independent firm. The contracts are tested and open to read; treat launches as experimental and risk only what you can lose.
