hero-wide.webp / hero-tall.webp — the photographs behind the headline.

Drop the file in here with exactly that name and it takes over the hero on
the next load; if it is missing, the page falls back to the drawn grove in
art.js. Nothing else needs changing.

Wanted: landscape, 2400x1400 or larger, under about 400 KB after
compression. The framing assumes the subject sits right of centre — the
left third is covered by a dark scrim so the headline stays legible
(styles.css, `.hero .frame::after`, and `object-position: 62% 50%`).

Rights: whatever goes here has to be licensed for this use. The Fuji and
Chureito Pagoda view is one of the most photographed in Japan and most of
the versions in circulation are rights-managed stock. Buy a licence, shoot
it, or take one from a source with a licence that permits commercial use
(Unsplash, Pexels, or a Wikimedia file whose page states CC BY or CC0) and
record which one in this file.
