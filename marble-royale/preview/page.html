    JOIN

    How to join a race

    No account, no password, no email. Your wallet is your login, and entering costs nothing.

    1. Open the page with your walletThe coin lives on Robinhood Chain, which any Ethereum wallet can reach: MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet or Trust Wallet. On a desktop the extension is enough. On a phone, open this page inside your wallet's own browser. From plain Safari or Chrome there is nothing to connect to.
    2. Connect and sign the messageYour wallet shows a short text with your address and a one-time number. Signing text is not a transaction: it moves no funds, grants no allowance over your tokens and costs no gas. It only proves the wallet is yours, so nobody can enter under someone else's address.
    3. Join the queueOne button, one marble. The queue closes a few seconds before the start; if you are late you are simply in the next race. One marble per wallet per race, and auto-join keeps you in every race without touching anything.
    4. Watch, and get paid if you winThe winning address appears in full on screen and stays in the log, so anyone can check afterwards that the payment was made.

    HOWWhat launching does›

    Your wallet deploys the coin through the launchpad contract, which sets the creator-fee split at deploy time: a slice of every trade goes to the coin's fee wallet, and that wallet is what the races draw their pot from. You keep the rest of the creator fees as with any launch.

    The moment the coin exists it gets its own page here: same track, same physics, same provably fair seed, but its own queue and its own pot. Holders of your coin race for your coin's fees. You set the cadence and the winner's share above, and you can change them later from your creator panel.

    COSTWhat it costs›

    Gas for the deployment, paid by your wallet, and the launchpad's own cut of the creator fees, fixed at launch and shown on this form before you sign anything. There is no listing fee and nothing to buy up front.

    PAYOUTWho pays the winners›

    For a launched coin the winner's share is set aside by the fee contract itself, so it does not rely on anyone remembering to send it. The winning address and the amount are on the coin's page and in the log, and the payout transaction is linked when it goes out.

    THE POT

    Where the money comes from

    The coin earns creator fees every time somebody buys or sells. The more the coin moves, the more fees land, and that is what the winner takes a share of.

    1

    Fees pile up

    Every trade on the coin sends creator fees, in ETH on Robinhood Chain, to one wallet. A busy five minutes fills it; a quiet five minutes barely moves it.

    2

    A race is called

    Every five minutes, on the clock, on a track drawn fresh for that race. The pot is what came in during that window, in dollars at the ETH price of the moment: three to seven dollars on a normal race. On the hour and the half hour it is a mega race with a $25 pot.

    3

    The winner takes 20%

    The creator sends that share to the winning address by hand. The percentage is theirs to set and it is always written on screen.

    There is no smart contract paying anyone automatically. A person sends it, which is worth knowing before you play. The guide further down says so plainly.

    PROVABLY FAIR

    Why it cannot be rigged

    Whoever picks the seed picks the winner, so the seed is nailed down before anyone knows who is playing.

    STEP 1Before anyone joins›

    The server draws a secret at random and publishes only sha256(secret). That hash is on screen from the first second of the round and it cannot be taken back.

    STEP 2When the queue closes›

    The seed is sha256(secret + the list of wallets), and the secret itself is published alongside it. The field is fixed at that moment, so it cannot be edited to suit the seed.

    STEP 3After the race›

    Anyone can check that the hash from the start really is the hash of that secret, and replay the whole race in their own browser from the seed. If the winner does not match, the server lied and it shows.

    ALSOStarting slots and cheering›

    Starting positions are shuffled with the same seed, so joining early buys you nothing. Cheers are confetti: if they could nudge a marble, the race would stop being the same race in every browser.

      GUIDE

      What you are getting into

      THE IMPORTANT PART

      Connecting your wallet and signing the message does not move your money and gives nobody any allowance over your tokens. Nobody here will ever ask for your seed phrase or private key. Anyone who does is robbing you.

      • ✓What connecting does: the site reads your public address, the same one anyone can see in an explorer, and asks for a text signature to check it is yours. Free, and not a transaction.
      • ✓What it never does: request token approvals, open transactions, touch your funds or store any key. The server holds no wallets. It reads public balances and nothing else.
      • ✓Entering is free. There is nothing to pay, nothing is staked, and you cannot lose money by racing a marble.
      • !Paying out is manual and voluntary. The creator sends it from their own wallet, at the percentage they choose. This site shows who won; it cannot force anyone to pay or refund anything.
      • !This is chance, not skill. The result comes out of a physics simulation with a random seed. Nobody rolls better than anyone else, and winning before does not help.
      • !A marble is free, and that has a limit: nothing stops one person entering with several wallets except requiring a minimum token balance to play.
      • !None of this is financial advice. A memecoin can lose all of its value in minutes. Only take part with what you can afford to lose, and only if you are old enough where you live.
      • !This page is a preview. The marbles you see are demonstrations and the pot is an invented number, there to show how it looks.
