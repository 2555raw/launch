RACES

Upcoming races

One race at a time, on the clock. You join each race yourself, from its lobby, while the queue is open.

GAME MODES

Eight tracks. The crowd picks the next one.

Every race draws a fresh course from its seed inside one of eight modes. When a race ends, the results screen puts two modes to a vote and the winner is the next track. Each picture below is drawn from the course generator itself, not an illustration.

HOW IT WORKS
  1. Connect a walletMetaMask, Phantom or any EVM wallet. Signing in is a message, never a transaction. A demo wallet is one tap away if you just want to look.
  2. Join the racePick a marble, pick a name, press join. One marble per wallet, one race at a time: nobody is entered into a race they did not join.
  3. Watch it runThe camera chases the leaders down a track of ramps, spinners, jumps and boost pads. Every browser sees the same race at the same moment.
  4. Win the potFirst over the line takes the whole prize pool, paid to the winning address. The result and the address stay in the log.
  5. Vote the next trackTwo modes go up on the results screen. One vote per wallet; the winner is the next race's track, and the lobby shows it with a picture.
PROVABLY FAIR

The seed is fixed before anyone joins

The server publishes sha256(secret) when a race opens. When the queue closes, the seed is sha256(secret + the field) and the secret is revealed. Anyone can replay the race from the seed and check the winner.
