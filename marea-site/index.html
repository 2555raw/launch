FAQ

Before you go live

Your wallet, your gas, your inventory. MAREA only does what your limits allow — and stops the moment you tell it to.

What is MAREA?

A two-sided quoting engine. It keeps a buy order and a sell order around the market price of a token you choose, and moves both as your inventory and the market's volatility change.

Which chains and venues are supported?

EVM chains with Uniswap v3 or v4 pools, Solana through Raydium CLMM and OpenBook, and launchpad tokens on either side while they are still on their bonding curve.

What do I need to go live?

A pair, a venue, a funded hot wallet and a spend limit. Until all four are set, the engine stays in simulation.

How do I stop everything?

The stop button cancels every open order and pulls liquidity from every venue in one go. It sits in the top bar and never moves.

Do my private keys ever reach the browser?

No. Keys stay on the engine host; the browser only receives status messages.

How do I sign in?

Connect an EVM or Solana wallet and sign a one-time message. No password, no email.

What should I tune first?

Start in simulation with a wide spread and a small inventory cap. Watch a few hours of fills, then tighten one setting at a time.
