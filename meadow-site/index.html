Meadow is a window onto Uniswap. It finds the best pool for your trade, shows you exactly what you'll get, and hands the transaction to your wallet.

1

Connect your wallet

MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet or any browser wallet. No sign-up. Your wallet is your account.

2

Features

Everything Meadow does

🔀

Best-pool routing

Every quote checks the deepest Uniswap v3 and v4 pools, direct or through ETH or USDG, and takes whichever pays you most.

🔐

Non-custodial

Meadow never holds your funds. Each swap is one transaction from your wallet through Uniswap's Universal Router, and the tokens land straight back in your wallet.

📈

Live prices

Every price on this page is read from the pools on Robinhood Chain. Nothing is hard-coded.

🛡️

Slippage protection

You set the worst price you'll accept. If the market moves past it, the swap reverts and you keep your tokens.

✅

Exact approvals

When you sell, Meadow approves exactly the amount you're swapping and signs a Permit2 permit that expires in 30 minutes. Never an unlimited allowance.

🧾

No platform fee

You pay the pool's own liquidity fee and the network fee. Meadow adds nothing on top.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Meadow is a swap interface for tokenized stocks on Robinhood Chain. It reads prices from Uniswap v3 and v4 pools and sends your swap to Uniswap's official Universal Router, signed by your own wallet.

Every tokenized stock that has live Uniswap liquidity against ETH or USDG on Robinhood Chain: Apple, NVIDIA, Tesla, Microsoft, Amazon, Alphabet, Meta and more. The list in Markets is the full set, and you can also swap one stock straight into another.

The pool's liquidity fee (shown before you swap, from 0.01% to 1% depending on the pool) and the Robinhood Chain network fee. Meadow charges no platform fee.

Selling a token goes through Permit2, Uniswap's approval contract. First you approve exactly the amount you're selling, then you sign a free permit (no gas) that lets the router move that amount for the next 30 minutes, then you confirm the swap. Buying with ETH is a single transaction.
