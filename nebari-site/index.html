About

Built for anyone planting a
token worth holding.

Nebari is a Robinhood Chain launchpad focused on one thing: launching a token that trades against any token on the chain, with the liquidity locked at launch, your fees paid in that asset, and a supply that only ever shrinks.

Non-custodial

Every transaction is signed from your own wallet. Nebari never holds your keys or your tokens, and the contracts have no owner and no admin keys.

Liquidity locked

The whole supply goes into a Uniswap v4 pool in the launch transaction and the contract has no function to take it out. Not you, not us, not ever.

Fees in the paired asset

Holders earn half of every swap fee in the asset the token is paired with, so a TSLA pair pays its holders in TSLA. Claim any time, straight from the contract.

Every sale prunes the supply

Fixed one billion supply, no mint function, a price floor at the start price. Fees the pool earns in the token itself are burned, so the supply only ever shrinks.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What does it mean to 'root' a token to an asset?

Your token launches into a Uniswap v4 pool priced in that asset. It does not track the asset's price and gives you no claim on it. The pairing decides what people trade against and which asset your fees arrive in.

Which assets can I pair against?

Any token on Robinhood Chain. The quick picks are Robinhood stock tokens such as TSLA, NVDA, AAPL and SPY, plus ETH and USDG. Or paste the contract address of any other token on the chain, a memecoin included.

What does it cost to launch?

Just gas. Nebari charges no launch fee and asks for no capital: the launch transaction creates the token and its pool at the same time, with the whole supply as the liquidity.

Can the liquidity be pulled?

No. The whole supply goes into the Uniswap v4 pool in the launch transaction, the factory contract owns the position, and it has no function to remove it. Not you, not us. The only thing that ever leaves the pool is the swap fees.

How do holders earn?

Half of every swap fee the pool earns in the pair asset is credited to the token's holders, in proportion to what they hold. A token paired with TSLA pays its holders in TSLA. Anyone can trigger the collection, and each holder claims from the Claim page. The rest goes to the creator and a small protocol share.

Is Nebari custodial?

No. Every transaction is signed from your own browser wallet. Nebari never holds your keys, your tokens or your fees, and the contracts have no owner.

Why is every token a bonsai?

Nebari (根張り) is the root spread of a bonsai, the part that holds everything up: here it is the locked liquidity. Each token gets a sakura bonsai grown from its own address, so no two look alike, and it fills out with blossoms as the pool's volume grows. Nothing is uploaded; it is drawn from the chain.
