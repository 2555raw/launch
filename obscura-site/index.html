Questions traders ask first.

0x01Does HeldAt ever hold my funds or keys?

No. There is no server in the loop. Cloaking, transfers and proofs run in your browser with the Web Crypto API, and receipts are stored on your device.

0x02What happens if I lose a receipt?

That bond can no longer be opened or proven, by you or anyone else. Export a backup from the vault and keep it somewhere safe.

0x03What does a counterparty learn when I prove a bond?

The asset and amount in that one proof, and that it was sealed and not edited. Nothing about your other bonds. If the proof is backed by a wallet, they also see that wallet’s address, and anything public about it. To keep the rest private, back the proof with a wallet that holds only what you want to show.

0x04Do I need to connect a wallet?

Not to try it. Sealing, the vault, transfers and checking proofs all work without one. A wallet does two things: it signs a proof so the other side can check the balance on the blockchain, and it can anchor a seal code onchain with a transaction to yourself. HeldAt never asks for your keys or a token approval.

0x05Is $HELD live?

Not yet. The token and the bond contract are not deployed. We will announce it on X; ignore any address you receive anywhere else.
