Questions traders ask first.

Does Obscura ever hold my funds or keys?

No. There is no server in the loop. Cloaking, transfers and proofs run in your browser with the Web Crypto API, and receipts are stored on your device.

What happens if I lose a receipt?

That bond can no longer be opened or proven, by you or anyone else. Export a backup from the vault and keep it somewhere safe.

What does a counterparty learn when I prove a bond?

The contents of that one receipt and whether it matches the commitment they expect. Nothing about your other bonds or your wallet.

Do I need to connect a wallet?

Only to anchor a commitment onchain, which sends a transaction to yourself with the 32-byte hash as data. Everything else works without one.

Is $OBX live?

Not yet. The token and the bond contract are not deployed. We will announce it on X; ignore any address you receive anywhere else.
