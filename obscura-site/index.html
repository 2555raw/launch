How it works

Cloak, hold, prove. All three run on your device.

01

Cloak

SHA-256 over the asset, a 16-byte salt and a 32-byte key. Anchor it and the chain stores 32 bytes.

02

Hold

The receipt lives in your browser's storage. Back it up: lose it and nobody can open that bond again.

03

Prove

Your counterparty pastes the receipt into Verify and gets a single bit back: true or false.
