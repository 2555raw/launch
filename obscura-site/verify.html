Verify a receipt

Someone handed you a receipt to prove they hold a bond. Paste it here. Your browser recomputes the hash and answers true or false.

Take it from the chain or from a channel you trust, not from the same message as the receipt. Without it you only learn that the receipt is self-consistent.
