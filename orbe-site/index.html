    How it works

    Three steps. That’s it.

    1. 01

      Connect your wallet

      Sign in with the wallet you already use. ORBE never touches your keys or holds your funds.

    2. 02

      Pick a pair

      The panel queries every network at once and ranks prices from best to worst, fees included.

    3. 03

      Execute where it pays

      One click routes the order to the venue with the best net result. You see the final cost before you confirm.

    Features

    Built for fast decisions.

    True net price

    We compare after fees, gas and estimated slippage. The number you see is the number you get.

    Non-custodial

    You sign every trade. No deposits, no held balances, no accounts to open.

    Price alerts

    Set a target and we’ll ping you when any network hits it, in the browser or on Telegram.

    Clean history

    Every order, on every network, in one table you can export to CSV.

    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions.

    Does ORBE hold my funds?

    No. ORBE only prepares the transaction. You sign it from your own wallet, and your funds never pass through us.

    How much does it cost?

    The panel is free. You only pay the network and venue fees for your order, and you always see them before you confirm.

    Which wallets are supported?

    Any WalletConnect-compatible wallet, plus the most popular browser extensions on each network.

    Can I use it on my phone?

    Yes. The app runs in your mobile browser and fits small screens without losing features.
