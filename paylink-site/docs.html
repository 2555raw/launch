How it works

PayLink routes creator fees from Pons tokens into a PayPal balance. That balance does not have to be yours: a coin can be launched on someone else's behalf and they are paid without ever holding a wallet. It deploys no smart contracts of its own and uses no payment processor, combining four things that already exist: the Pons fee escrow, the Across bridge, a Curve pool, and the fact that PayPal accepts its own stablecoin from any wallet.

1. The fee address

Every Pons launch names a creator fee recipient. Fees accrue inside the Pons escrow under that address, denominated in whatever asset the token trades against. PayLink gives you an address it controls, one per link, derived from a single key. Because the escrow tracks balances per address, the balance under your address is exactly your fees. Nothing is pooled.

2. Claiming

A worker checks the escrow every few minutes. Once your balance passes a dollar, it claims the USDG, moves it to the treasury, and records the transaction. 80% is credited to your balance and 20% is the platform share. Waiting for a dollar keeps gas from eating small payouts.

3. Becoming dollars

USDG is bridged to USDC on Ethereum through Across, then swapped to PYUSD on Curve. Both legs are public and permissionless. This keeps a PYUSD float on Ethereum that payouts draw from, which means a slow bridge never delays your payout.

4. Getting paid

When your balance passes $5, the next run sends PYUSD to the address you gave. Inside PayPal it appears under crypto as PYUSD and converts to your dollar balance one to one. A transfer that fails is credited straight back to you and retried.

5. The platform share

The remaining 20% is forwarded as USDG to a single published address, listed on the fees page along with every transfer that has ever gone to it. There is no treasury to audit and no platform token involved.

What can go wrong

!We hold your fees briefly

Between the claim and the payout the money sits in wallets PayLink controls. Payouts are batched, so that window is usually hours. Every movement is a public transaction.

!A wrong address loses the money

PYUSD must go to an Ethereum address. If you copy a Solana address, or any address you do not control, it cannot be recovered by us or anyone.

!Only USDG pairs are paid out

A token paired with ETH accrues fees in ETH, which PayLink does not convert yet. Those fees stay in the Pons escrow, claimable by you at any time.

!Outages pause payouts, they do not lose money

If the bridge or the pool is down, the float stops being replenished and payouts wait. Your balance is in the ledger the whole time.

!This is taxable income

PayLink issues no tax forms. PayPal applies its own rules to crypto you receive. That is between you and them.
