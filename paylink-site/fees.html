What PayLink charges

Of every fee we route, 80% goes to the creator and 20% is ours. That is the whole pricing model. There is no listing fee, no withdrawal fee, and no spread taken on the conversion. Network and bridge costs come out of our share, not yours.

Our share is forwarded as USDG to one address, published below. Every transfer to it is listed on this page, so what we have taken is a number you can check rather than one you have to believe.

Why USDG and not dollars

Pons pays creator fees in whatever asset a token trades against, and PayLink only supports USDG-paired launches, so the fees arrive as USDG. The creator's side gets bridged and converted into PYUSD for them. Our side is not converted at all; it is forwarded as it arrived, which is one less moving part and one less thing to get wrong.
