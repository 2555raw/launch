What you can do with it

You are paid in PYUSD, which is PayPal's own dollar. Once it is in your account it stops behaving like crypto and starts behaving like the balance you already had.

$

Turn it into dollars

Convert PYUSD to your USD balance one to one, with no conversion fee. From there it behaves like any other PayPal money: spend it or move it to your bank.

⟶

Pay a person

Send it to anyone else on PayPal. Splitting earnings with a co-founder, paying an artist who drew your art, sending your mum some of it. Free between US friends and family.

◎

Pay a business

Send it to a business's PayPal, or check out with it directly. PayPal sells the PYUSD to cover the purchase, and the order still gets Purchase Protection.

▤

Buy things

Spend it at the millions of US merchants that already take PayPal. Nothing about the checkout changes; you just pick PYUSD as the source.

Straight answers

Can I pay someone who has never heard of crypto?

Yes, and that is the point. They send you one address from their PayPal app and never touch anything again. No wallet, no seed phrase, no gas, no account here. Dollars simply arrive in an app they already have.

Is this actually spendable, or just crypto in an app?

Spendable. PYUSD converts to your PayPal dollar balance one to one with no fee, and PayPal lets you check out with it at any US merchant that takes PayPal. It is not a token you have to go find an exchange for.

Do I need a business account or any paperwork?

No. You need a personal PayPal account with crypto turned on, which is a few taps. There is no application, no approval, and no identity check run by us.

Do I have to trust you with the money?

Briefly, yes. Between claiming a fee from Pons and sending your PYUSD, we hold it. Payouts run on a schedule, and every claim, swap, bridge and payout is a public transaction you can open in a block explorer.

Can I take my fees back?

At any time, without asking us. You point the Pons fee recipient wherever you like with one call to the Pons factory. We never hold your token and cannot stop you.

What does it cost?

20% of routed fees. Network and bridge costs come out of our side, not yours, so the number you see is the number you get.

What if nobody trades my token?

Then there are no fees and you have paid the Pons launch fee and nothing else. Fees only exist when people trade. Most tokens earn very little.

Read this before you launch
  • We hold your fees briefly. Between claiming from Pons and sending PYUSD, the money sits in wallets PayLink controls. Payouts go out once your balance passes $5.00.
  • A wrong address loses the money. PYUSD must go to an Ethereum address. Pick the Ethereum network in your app, not Solana.
  • USDG pairs only. Tokens paired with ETH are not paid out; their fees would sit in the Pons escrow untouched.
  • This is income. PayLink issues no tax forms, and PayPal applies its own rules to crypto you receive.
  • Most tokens earn nothing. Fees only exist if people trade. The calculator above is arithmetic, not a forecast.
