What happens after you sign

The token is paired with USDG and its creator fee recipient is the PayPal address you typed, set directly in the Pons factory. Creator fees accrue in the Pons escrow under that address. You can point them anywhere else at any time with one call to the factory, from the Link a token page. The automatic bridge to PYUSD is not running yet, so what reaches PayPal today is whatever that address claims and moves itself; the proof page shows the live state of every piece.
