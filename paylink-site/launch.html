What happens after you sign

The token is paired with USDG and its creator fee recipient is an address PayLink controls, one per launch. Every few minutes the fees are claimed, 80% is bridged and swapped into PYUSD and sent to the address above, and 20% is forwarded as USDG to the published platform address. You can point the fees back to yourself at any time with one call to the Pons factory.
