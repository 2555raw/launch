FAQ

Questions, answered

Does Peg custody my funds?

No. Every trade settles wallet-to-wallet through audited contracts, and approvals are scoped per order. Peg cannot move your balance.

What does it cost?

There is no platform fee on spot routes. Solvers compete on price, and the quote you see is the total: venue fees, price impact and gas included.

Which chains are supported?

Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum and Solana at launch, with the same routing engine and one shared account across all of them.

Can I trade size without moving the market?

Yes. Orders above $250k route through RFQ makers and TWAP schedules; the desk can also work a block quote for you directly.

Is there an API?

Yes, REST plus WebSocket with the same rate limits for everyone. Keys are self-serve from the dashboard once you have traded once.
