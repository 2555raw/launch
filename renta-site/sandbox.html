    What is real here, and what is not

    • The rules are the contract’s. Price is what the vault holds divided by the shares in issue. A deposit mints at that price. A redemption pays it minus the curve, 3% on the day of a deposit falling to nothing at 90 days, and the curve stays in the vault for everyone else. A redemption the reserve cannot cover waits for the next close.
    • Time is a month per close. Each close moves the calendar forward a month, so the curve is 3%, 2%, 1%, then gone, depending on how many closes have passed since your last deposit.
    • The rent is simulated. The vault starts where the real Rolls end. From then on each close collects between 90% and 100% of the scheduled rent and pays 26% to 36% of it in costs, fixed for each month so everyone sees the same forecast.
    • Nothing is money. EURG here comes from a tap, a wallet signs a message and pays no gas, and nothing touches a chain. The real vault, when it goes live, is the contract in the docs.

    RENTA is a demonstration build. The sandbox is a game with play money, and its figures say nothing about what a real vault would do. Nothing here is investment advice.
