FAQ

Everything you need to know: models, funding, privacy and what it costs.

What is Wondr?

Wondr is a single platform where you can access every major AI model for text, code, images and voice, paying only for what you use.

Do I need a subscription?

No. There is nothing recurring. You fund one balance and it goes down as you use models. When it runs out you top up, and only then.

How do I pay?

With crypto from the wallet you already use: USDT, BTC, ETH or SOL, at the live rate when you fund. Your deposit is converted to credits at $1 = 1,000 credits with no wondr fee on the deposit.

What models are available?

Chat and code with Claude, GPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, Grok and open models like Llama, Qwen and Mistral. Images with FLUX and Nano Banana. Video is coming soon. Voice with GPT-4o TTS and ElevenLabs. New models are added as they ship, and $WONDR holders get them 14 days early.

How much does it cost?

Each model has a transparent per-request price on the pricing page, metered against your balance as you go: per token for text, per image. The calculator turns your workload into credits. $WONDR holders pay 5% of the list price within a daily allowance.

Is my data private?

There is no sign-up form and email is optional: sign in with a username, a wallet signature or an email code. Prompts go to the model provider you chose and nowhere else. Your chats and library stay on your account until you delete them.

Do my credits expire?

No. Credits stay on your balance until you spend them.
