Models & pricing.

Every model on the platform, with its list price and the $SEEKR holder price. $1 buys 1,000 credits and each request is metered against your balance: per token for text, per image, per second of video, per character of speech.

Compare

The same prompt can go to any chat model in the picker, so comparing is a matter of asking twice. Prices update as providers change them; the list price carries a small margin over the provider's own price (see the methodology).

Alternatives

A subscription to each provider costs $20 to $200 a month each, whether you use it or not. Here, a light month can cost a dollar and a heavy one costs what it costs. The calculator shows the number for your workload.
