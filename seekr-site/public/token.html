Pay 5%

Every model is available at 5% of its list price, on a daily allowance that scales with what you hold: 1,000 credits per 0.01% of supply, up to 25,000 credits a day. The allowance resets at 00:00 UTC. Anything past it is list price, so holding more never costs more.

Supply

1,000,000,000 tokens, fixed. No mint, no presale, no team allocation. Everything goes onto the Pons curve at creation; the company buys its 5% there like anyone else and locks it.

Chain

Robinhood Chain, an Arbitrum L2 with chain id 4663, paired against ETH. Liquidity is locked with Team Finance when the curve graduates.

How holdings are checked

Link a wallet in your account: it signs a message (no transaction) and the server reads your $WONDR balance from the chain. The percentage sets your allowance from that moment; refresh it any time.
