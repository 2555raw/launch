Questions

Frequently asked

Can a spin be repeated if the colour is unwanted?

No. The spin is spent the moment it is taken and stays bound to that draft. A draft can be discarded, which clears the name, ticker, supply and description along with the spin — that is starting over, not re-rolling.

Can a coin be launched without spinning?

No. The launch control ships disabled, the step machine only reaches the launch step with a resolved spin, and the launch routine checks the spin again before it writes anything. Re-enabling the control from a console produces no launch.

Is the dial fair?

The sixteen dots are evenly spaced and the outcome comes from crypto.getRandomValues rather than Math.random. Every asset holds exactly one dot, so each is drawn 6.25% of the time and each colour family 25%. The proof section reports the observed split.

What does the quadrant do?

It is half the answer. The colour picks one of four asset families and the quadrant — bid hand, ask hand, long leg, short leg — picks which of the four names inside that family you get: a red dot on the bid hand and a red dot on the short leg are different tokens. Sixteen dots on the board, sixteen tokens, one to a dot — the whole table is on the asset desk, and every square is checked against the chain before it can be launched against.

Does this issue real tokens?

Yes. Launching deploys a real ERC-20 on Base from your own wallet, and the draw — the asset, the colour family, the quadrant and who spun — is written into the contract at construction, with no function anywhere that can change it afterwards. The pad keeps a local list of what you launched; the chain keeps the real one.

Before you step on the mat

This is a game. The money is not.

Spinpad is a board game with a real contract at the end of it. The spinning, the mat and the figure are the game; what the pad does when you press launch is a transaction on Base that nobody can take back. The short version, so nobody is surprised later:

  • Launching deploys a real ERC-20 on Base mainnet. It costs gas, it is permanent and it happens from your address. There is no undo, no support desk and no refund.
  • You do not pick the asset your coin is paired with. A spinner does. That is not a bug we are working on, it is the entire product.
  • Opening a pool moves real funds out of your wallet. It is optional, it is a separate confirmation, and a new pool with thin liquidity is trivially easy for anyone to drain.
  • Nothing here is an investment and nobody here is advising you. A token made in an afternoon is worth whatever someone will pay for it, which is usually nothing.
  • Check the addresses yourself. The pad reads its own token list back off the chain and refuses anything that does not match, but the list in config.js is whatever was put there. Read it before you trust it.

The mat is honest. It is still the only thing here that is.
