Spinpad assigns the underlying asset with a single spin taken before launch. Four colours, four assets, one spin per token. The result is written into the launch record the moment the dial stops, and from then on it cannot be edited, re-rolled or overridden.

How it works

  1. 1

    Token details

    Name, ticker, total supply and a one-line description. The asset field stays empty.

  2. 2

    Mandatory spin

    One spin per launch, taken before anything is created. The dial is not skippable.

  3. 3

    Asset locked

    The colour drawn fills the underlying asset field and closes it to editing.

  4. 4

    Launch

    The launch button opens and the token ships with its spin record attached.

Frequently asked

Can a spin be repeated if the colour is unwanted?

No. The spin is spent the moment it is taken and stays bound to that draft. A draft can be discarded, which clears the name, ticker, supply and description along with the spin — that is starting over, not re-rolling.

Can a token be launched without spinning?

No. The launch control ships disabled, the step machine only reaches the launch step with a resolved spin, and the launch routine checks the spin again before it writes anything. Re-enabling the control from the console does not produce a launch.

Is the dial fair?

The sixteen sectors are equal and the outcome comes from crypto.getRandomValues rather than Math.random. Each colour holds exactly four sectors, so the expected share is 25%. The distribution section above reports the observed split.

What does the quadrant do?

The colour governs the outcome: it sets the underlying asset. The quadrant — right hand, left hand, right foot, left foot — is recorded alongside it as part of the spin record and affects no parameter of the token.

Does this issue real tokens?

No. Spinpad is a working simulation of the mechanic. There is no chain, no contract and no money; market caps, replies and curve progress are generated figures. Launches are stored in this browser only.
