Read every line

            
Every hook comes as plain Solidity you can read, and it is compiled in your browser, so the bytecode you deploy comes from the code on your screen. Each template is tested against Uniswap V4's own PoolManager.

          

          

            
You own what you deploy

            
Hooks and pools are created by your wallet, directly on Uniswap V4's PoolManager. They keep working whether or not you ever open UnyHooks again.

          

          

            
Built for Robinhood Chain

            
Hooks and pools deploy to Robinhood Chain, on its Uniswap V4 PoolManager. Generated hooks are not audited and this is a live network, so get a hook reviewed before you trust it with real funds.

Questions

        

          

            What is a Uniswap V4 hook?
            
A hook is a small contract attached to a Uniswap V4 pool. The pool calls it at set moments (before and after swaps, or when liquidity is added and removed), so the hook can change fees, take a cut, block a trade or run any other rule you give it.

          

          

            Do I need to know Solidity?
            
No. You describe the behaviour and UnyHooks writes the contract. The full source is always there if you or a reviewer want to read it.

          

          

            What do I need to get started?
            
A wallet that works with Robinhood Chain and a little ETH there for gas. You sign in with a message, and only deploys and pool creation cost gas.

          

          

            Which tokens can I build pools with?
            
Any token on Robinhood Chain: your own, ETH, USDG, and Robinhood stock tokens such as NVDA, TSLA and SPY.

          

          

            Are the hooks safe to use with real money?
            
Generated hooks are not audited. They compile and follow well-known patterns, but this is a live network: have a hook reviewed before you put real funds behind it, and start with small amounts.

          

          

            Can I add liquidity from the chat?
            
Yes. Ask to add or remove liquidity and UnyHooks prepares the transaction; your wallet shows it to you and nothing happens until you sign.
