Your recovery phrase

These 12 words are your wallet. Copy them onto paper, in order. If you lose them, nobody can give them back to you.

Never put it in a chat, a photo or an email. Nobody legitimate will ever ask you for it.

      Launch a coin

      Your coin gets a price the moment it exists, set by a curve rather than by you: every buy moves it up, every sell moves it down. Once enough has been bought it graduates to a real pool and trades anywhere.

      You need a wallet first Launching signs a transaction, so there has to be a key to sign it with. Creating one takes about a minute and never leaves this browser.
      Launching happens on Solana The curve program runs there. Switch network to continue. Your wallet already has a Solana account on the same twelve words.
      Ward's launchpad is not switched on yet A launchpad needs a configuration on chain that fixes the curve, the fees and where a graduated coin goes. It is a one-time transaction and it has not been made. Everything below is built and checked, and nothing can be launched until it exists.

      Before you do this
      • It cannot be undone, edited or taken down. The coin exists the moment the transaction lands.
      • Anyone can buy and sell it immediately, including people betting against you.
      • Most coins launched this way never find buyers. Put in what you are willing to put in.
      • Selling a coin you created may be regulated where you live. That is on you, not on Ward.
