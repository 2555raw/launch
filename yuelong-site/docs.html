What Yuelong is

Yuelong is a front end for creating and trading tokens that orbit the Bittensor economy, on two EVM networks: Robinhood Chain and Bittensor EVM. It reads subnet data from Bittensor and token data from the EVM side, and never mixes the two up: a token launched here is not a subnet and not an alpha token.

From one place you can launch a coin paired with TAO, trade it on its bonding curve and then in its pool, and browse every launch.

Launching

One transaction from your wallet creates the coin and its bonding curve, on either network. The whole supply of 1,000,000,000 sits on the curve; nobody, the creator included, gets an allocation. The price starts from a virtual reserve and rises with every buy.

You can add a first buy to the same transaction. It lands before anyone else can trade and pays no snipe tax, so it is the fair way for a creator to hold some of their own coin. On a native-TAO pair you simply send the TAO along with the launch.

When the curve has raised its target, the buy that crosses the line graduates it in the same transaction: the TAO it raised and the matching share of the remaining coins seed a pool at the curve's last price, and the coins left over are burned. The pool has no LP tokens, so its liquidity can never be pulled.

  • Fixed supply of 1,000,000,000, 18 decimals, no owner, no mint, no tax in the token itself.
  • A launch fee read from the factory and shown before you sign (it may be zero), plus gas.
  • An optional creator fee of up to 2% of every curve trade, paid to the creator's wallet.

Coins, candidates, theses

Subnet coins point at an existing subnet: the creator names its NetUID. Candidates are for subnets that don't exist yet. TAO Ecosystem coins are about the network as a whole, or a sector of it. The label, the thesis, the image and the links are written into the launch transaction and stay on chain as they were.

None of these is subnet alpha. A subnet coin is a separate token that points at a subnet; it gives no stake, emissions or rights in it.

Trading

Every coin trades through one router, in native TAO: on its curve until it graduates, in its pool after that, from the same page and the same button. Each quote is read from the contracts just before you sign, and the router enforces your minimum: if the price moves past your slippage limit (1%, 3% or 10%) the trade reverts and nothing is spent but gas.

  • Curve trades pay a 1% protocol fee plus the coin's creator fee, if it has one.
  • Buys in the first 60 blocks after a launch pay a snipe tax that falls from 20% to 0. It stays in the curve, raising the floor for everyone who already holds.
  • Pool trades pay 0.3%, which stays in the pool.
  • Selling asks you to approve the exact amount you sell, never an unlimited allowance.

Pairs, gas and settlement

A market settles in whatever it's paired with. On Bittensor EVM that is native TAO: the router wraps it for you on the way in and unwraps it on the way out, so you never hold a wrapped token unless you want to. Gas is paid in TAO on Bittensor EVM and in ETH on Robinhood Chain. Dollar figures anywhere in the app are for orientation only.

Subnet data

Alpha prices, TAO reserves and volumes come from live subnet feeds, with a second source on standby. If both are down, the value is shown as unavailable. It is never filled in with an estimate.

Wallets

There's no account. Connect any injected EVM wallet (MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet, OKX Wallet and the like), let the app add the right network, and sign in your wallet. Watchlists are kept in your browser. The app never asks for a seed phrase or private key; if something does, it isn't us.

Security

Audit status. The contracts have a full test suite but have not yet been audited by an independent firm. When an audit is done, the report will be linked here in full, including any findings that were not fixed.

Reporting a vulnerability. Please report security issues privately rather than in public channels, with enough detail to reproduce them. Give us a reasonable time to fix the issue before disclosing it.

What we will never do. Ask for your seed phrase or private key, contact you first by direct message, or ask you to approve an unlimited token allowance.

Risk

Launch tokens are highly speculative and most go to zero. Contracts can have bugs, bridges can stall and prices can move faster than a quote. The code has been tested but has not yet had an independent audit. Only use what you can afford to lose.
