Before you sign

Is the token I launch subnet alpha?

No. You're launching your own ERC-20 that trades against TAO. It doesn't register a subnet or receive emissions.

What actually happens when I launch?

One transaction creates the coin, writes its metadata and opens its bonding curve. You can add a first buy to the same transaction, before anyone else can trade.

How much does it cost?

Network gas and the launch fee shown before you sign. Curve trades pay 1%, plus a creator fee of up to 2% if the creator set one.

Can anyone pull the liquidity?

No. When a curve graduates, its pool is seeded with no LP tokens at all, so nobody, the creator and Yuelong included, can take the liquidity out.

Do I need to sign up?

No account and no email. Your wallet is your login.

What does "graduation" mean?

Every coin starts on a bonding curve. When the curve has raised its target, the TAO and the matching share of the unsold supply move into a pool in the same transaction, at the curve's last price, and the rest of the supply is burned. Trading carries on in the pool from the same page.

Which wallets work?

Any EVM wallet that can add a custom network: MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet and OKX Wallet are the ones we test with. The app offers to add Robinhood Chain or Bittensor EVM for you.

Where are the contracts?

Contract addresses are listed on the Security section of the docs, together with how to verify them on a block explorer. Always check the address in your wallet before you sign.

Will Yuelong ever DM me or ask for my seed phrase?

Never. We don't run support by direct message and nothing on this site asks for a seed phrase or private key. Anyone who does is not us.

How do I report a bug or a scam token?

See Security in the docs for how to reach us. For vulnerabilities, please report privately before sharing anything publicly.

Has the code been audited?

Not yet by an independent firm. The contracts are tested and open to read; treat launches as experimental and risk only what you can lose.
