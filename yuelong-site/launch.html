Summary

Anyone can buy or sell against the curve from the first block. Buys in the first 60 blocks pay a snipe tax that falls from 20% to 0 and stays in the curve. Graduation happens on its own when the target is reached.

Graduated liquidity is locked permanently. The contracts are tested but have not had an independent audit yet.
