Privacy, plainly.

No accounts, no emails. Here is what is and isn't collected.

What we collectBlockchain dataThird partiesHow we use itYour choicesContact

What we collect

Yuelong has no sign-up. When you connect a wallet, the website sees your public address, as any dapp does. Your browser keeps a few preferences locally (for example whether you accepted these terms); they stay on your device.

Our hosting provider records standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to keep the site running and secure.

Blockchain data

Transactions you sign are recorded on public blockchains. They are visible to anyone, permanently, and are not controlled by Yuelong, so we can't delete or change them.

Third parties

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and reads chain data from RPC providers and indexers; they may receive your IP address as part of those requests. Wallets and bridges you use are separate services with their own policies.

How we use it

Only to run, secure and improve the website. We don't sell personal data and don't use it for advertising.

Your choices

You can clear locally stored preferences at any time from your browser settings. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access or delete personal data we hold; contact us and we will help where we can.

Contact

Questions about this policy: reach us through the links in the site footer.
